About me

I’m passionate about Accounting, Technology, Creativity, and continuous learning. With a background in Commerce, I have recently become an AAT Sri Lanka Passed Finalist and am currently pursuing the Business Level at CA Sri Lanka.

I believe that effective time management, discipline, and a positive attitude help me become the best version of myself. I’m always eager to explore new technologies, develop new skills, and connect with people who bring new ideas and perspectives.

I believe that learning never ends. There’s always something new to discover, and perfection isn’t the goal. Mistakes are part of the journey, and every mistake brings a lesson that helps me grow and move forward. That’s why I keep challenging myself, learning new things, and developing my skills.

My goal is to shape my own future by combining the knowledge I’ve gained, the knowledge I will continue to gain, and the creativity within me. I want to keep growing, turn my ideas into reality, and create my own path forward.
